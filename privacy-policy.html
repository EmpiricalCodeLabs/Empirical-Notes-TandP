1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Empirical Notes mobile application ("the App") handles information. The App is provided by Empirical Code Labs ("we," "us," "our"), the trade name of E.B. van Wyk, registered with the Netherlands Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number 42206738. E.B. van Wyk is the controller for the processing described in sections 5.2 to 5.4. You can contact us at empiricalcodelabs@gmail.com.

In short: the App has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything you create is stored on your device, and we do not operate servers that receive it. A small number of features contact third-party services, as described in section 3.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

2.1 What the App Stores

When you use the App, the following is stored locally on your device:

  • Photos and videos you capture
  • Voice notes and video sound you record
  • Text notes, template data, projects, and custom templates you create
  • GPS location (coordinates, altitude, accuracy) at the time of capture
  • Addresses looked up for your capture locations
  • Device sensor data (compass heading, tilt, roll) at the time of capture
  • App settings and preferences, including when you accepted these documents
  • Street map images (OpenStreetMap) downloaded for the map view, kept in a cache

2.2 We Cannot Access It

This information never reaches us. We cannot view, recover, or delete it remotely. It stays on your device until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.

3. Information That Leaves Your Device

3.1 Address Lookup (Reverse Geocoding)

When you capture a photo or video while location is enabled and you are online, the App sends the GPS coordinates to your device's built-in geocoding service to find the nearest address. On Android this service is provided by Google, and on iOS by Apple. They process this request under their own privacy policies. If you are offline or have denied location permission, no lookup takes place. Only the coordinates are sent, not your photos, notes, or other content.

3.2 Map Tiles

When you open the map, the App downloads street map images from OpenStreetMap (operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation). If you switch on satellite view, imagery and a small amount of map information are downloaded from Esri's ArcGIS Location Platform, a service Esri provides to us; these requests include a key that identifies the App, not you. Like any website, these servers receive your IP address, the map area and zoom level being requested, and an identifier of the App. Your saved observations and their locations are not sent. Street map images are kept in a cache on your device and reused while they are current, so areas you have already viewed are not downloaded again. Satellite imagery is not stored on your device; it is only kept in memory while the map is open.

3.3 Sharing and Exporting

Content only leaves your device in other ways when you choose to export or share it. When you do:

  • Shared photos may contain location information in a watermark (if enabled in settings) or in the file's metadata
  • Exported project files contain your photos, videos, voice notes, notes, and location data, and are not encrypted
  • Content is sent through the app or service you choose (such as email or messaging apps), which has its own privacy policy
  • We have no control over how recipients or those services use the content

3.4 Files You Import

Project files you import, or share to the App from other apps, are copied into the App's local storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

3.5 App Store Statistics

Google Play gives us statistics about installations, and crash and performance reports. Google collects these, under its own privacy policy, from devices whose users have allowed usage and diagnostics data to be shared with Google. We receive them in aggregated form or without details that identify you, and they never include your content. We use them only to find and fix problems in the App.

4. How Information Is Used

4.1 Purposes

The information stored by the App is used on your device only to:

  • Organise and display your projects and observations
  • Show your captures on the map and calculate distances and areas
  • Add watermarks to photos, using your settings
  • Play back voice notes and videos
  • Export, import, and share project data when you ask it to

4.2 No Analytics, Advertising, Tracking, or Profiling

The App does not contain analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share information for advertising. We do not create profiles of you or make automated decisions about you.

5. Legal Basis (GDPR)

5.1 Data on Your Device

Because information stored by the App stays on your device and is not accessible to us, we do not process it as a controller. You decide what is recorded, kept, shared, and deleted.

5.2 Third-Party Requests

The address lookup and street map requests described in section 3 are made so that the features you use can work. Google, Apple, and the OpenStreetMap Foundation process this data as independent controllers under their own privacy policies. Satellite imagery works differently: Esri processes those requests (your IP address and the map area requested) on our behalf, as our processor under its Data Processing Addendum with us, so for this processing we are the controller. It is used only to show you the satellite view you switch on, which is necessary to provide that feature (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not receive this data ourselves. Esri may not use it for its own purposes, keeps it only as long as needed to provide the service to us, and returns or deletes it when our agreement ends.

5.3 Contacting Us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We do this based on our legitimate interest in answering your questions (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Our email account is hosted by Google (Gmail), so Google stores the correspondence. We delete it no later than two years after our last contact, unless we need it longer to deal with a legal claim.

5.4 App Store Statistics

Where the statistics and reports described in section 3.5 contain personal data, we use them based on our legitimate interest in keeping the App working and secure (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google keeps them for the periods set out in its own policies.

6. International Transfers

Some third-party providers in section 3 may process the data they receive outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. The OpenStreetMap Foundation is based in the United Kingdom, which the European Commission recognises as providing adequate protection. Google and Apple state that they rely on safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses for such transfers; please see their privacy policies for details. Esri processes satellite requests for us in the United States. Esri is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and its Data Processing Addendum with us adds the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses where they are needed. Our email provider (Google) and our website host (GitHub, see section 11) may also store data in the United States; both are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

7. Storage and Security

7.1 How Data Is Stored

The App uses the following storage on your device. All of it is needed for the App to work; none of it is used for tracking, and the App does not use cookies or advertising identifiers:

  • A local SQLite database for projects, observations, and templates
  • The App's private storage folder for photos, videos, thumbnails, and voice notes
  • Local preferences storage for settings
  • The App's cache folder for downloaded street map images, which your device may clear at any time

7.2 Security

Data in the App's storage is protected by your device's own security. Map and address requests are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The App does not add its own encryption, and exported files are not encrypted. No storage method is 100% secure. We recommend using a screen lock, keeping device encryption switched on, and storing exported files carefully.

7.3 Retention

Data is kept until you delete it in the App, clear the App's data in your device settings, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling permanently deletes everything that has not been exported.

8. Recording Other People

If you photograph, film, or record identifiable people, or use the App to document work for an employer or client, you may be responsible under data protection law for that information. Please respect the privacy of others, inform people where required, and share recordings only when you are allowed to.

9. Your Rights and Choices

9.1 Controls in the App and on Your Device

Because your data is on your device, you can exercise control over it directly:

  • View, edit, and delete individual photos, videos, voice notes, observations, or whole projects
  • Export your data in a portable format (ZIP project file and CSV)
  • Deny or revoke camera, location, and microphone permissions in your device settings
  • Turn off location watermarks in the App's settings
  • Clear all App data in your device settings, or uninstall the App

9.2 Your Rights Under the GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal data we hold about you, and the right to data portability. We honour these requests wherever you live. In practice, the only personal data we hold ourselves is email correspondence with us. Satellite map requests are processed for us by Esri (see section 5.2); because the App has no accounts, we cannot link them to you (Article 11 GDPR). To exercise your rights, contact empiricalcodelabs@gmail.com. We reply within one month, free of charge. For data held by Google, Apple, the OpenStreetMap Foundation, or GitHub, please contact those providers directly.

9.3 Complaints

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl). You can also contact the authority in your own country of residence. In the United Kingdom, this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly receive personal information from children. Since all data is stored on the device, parents and guardians are responsible for supervising their children's use of the device and the apps installed on it.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Our Website

GitHub records visitors' IP addresses to keep its service secure, as an independent controller under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The date at the top shows when it was last updated. Earlier versions are available from us on request.
