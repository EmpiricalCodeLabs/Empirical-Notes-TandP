2. Description of the App

2.1 Features

The App lets you document field work on your device. Its features include:

  • Capturing photos and videos with GPS location, compass heading, and device tilt
  • Recording voice notes and writing text notes
  • Organising observations into projects using built-in or custom templates
  • Viewing observation locations on maps and measuring distances and areas

  • Exporting, importing, and sharing project data
